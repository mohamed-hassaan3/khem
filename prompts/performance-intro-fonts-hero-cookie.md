# Mobile speed: intro, fonts, below-the-fold film, cookie card

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-19 as decision 6 of
`src/docs/production-audit.md` (§6 items 1–3, M4).

## Goal

Lift mobile Lighthouse Performance from 59–74 toward 80+, and show real content
sooner on a first visit, **without changing how KHEM looks once it has loaded**.
There are four changes. Each one is independent and can be reverted alone.

## Skills read

- `AGENTS.md` (design language, workflow, motion rules: ease-out, no bounce).
- `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` (`next/font/local` options, `next/image`).
- No clerk/supabase/ai-sdk change, so those skills are not needed.

  `MIN_HOLD_MS = 2200` and `MAX_HOLD_MS = 1000` are counted from the

## Decisions and assumptions

1. **The intro plays on the home page only** (`/` and `/ar`), still once per
   tab session. Any other landing page shows its content immediately and marks
   the intro as seen, so a later visit to home in the same tab does not play it
   either. The choreography itself (logo, bloom, gold rule, curtain parting) is
   unchanged.
2. **The intro clock starts at navigation, not hydration.** The floor becomes
   `max(0, MIN_HOLD_MS − performance.now())` at effect time, and
   `MIN_HOLD_MS` drops from 2200 to **1600**. `MAX_HOLD_MS` stays at 1000;
   `EXIT_MS` stays at 1100. The CSS timings are compressed proportionally so
   the entrance still completes before the exit begins (no half-played
   sequence). Reduced-motion values are unchanged.
3. **Fonts are subset, not replaced.** The same families, outlines and names.
   - Inter keeps `wght` and drops `opsz` (pinned at its default). It is limited
     to Basic Latin, Latin-1 Supplement, Latin Extended-A, General Punctuation,
     currency symbols (incl. €, £, ج.م is Arabic-script and not needed here),
     arrows and the few symbols the UI uses (◆ · → ✓ ×). The exact set is
     generated by scanning the EN dictionary, the components and the live EN
     pages for characters actually used, and taking the union with those ranges.
   - Amiri and IBM Plex Sans Arabic are limited to Arabic (U+0600–06FF,
     U+0750–077F, U+FB50–FDFF, U+FE70–FEFF), Basic Latin, digits and
     punctuation.
   - Cinzel is left alone (48 KB).
   - `src/app/fonts/README.md` records the exact `pyftsubset` commands and
     unicode ranges, so the files can be regenerated from upstream.
4. **The New Arrival film loads when it nears the viewport.** A small client
   component, `src/components/home/InViewVideo.tsx`, renders the same `<video>`
   with `preload="none"`, no `autoPlay` attribute, and the poster. An
   `IntersectionObserver` (`rootMargin: "200px"`) calls `play()` once the film
   is close and pauses it when it leaves. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it never
   autoplays (the poster stays), matching the hero's rule. No layout change, so
   no CLS.
5. **The cookie card gets smaller on phones, and stays away from the buy button
   on desktop.**
   - **Phones:** the short copy is already used. The two buttons go side by side
     (equal width), and "Manage preferences" stays collapsed by default. The
     target is ≤ 30% of a 390×844 screen.
   - **Desktop:** `sm:max-w-sm` (was `max-w-md`). The card stays at the bottom
     end and no longer reaches the product page's buy column at 1280×577.
     Verify with a screenshot; if it still overlaps, pad the product page's
     sticky buy area instead of moving the card.
   - Wording, colours, the gold hairline, focus handling and consent logic are
     untouched.

## Implementation requirements

- Server Components stay server. Only `InViewVideo` and the existing
  `IntroCurtain`/`CookieConsent` are client components.
- No new dependencies. fonttools runs from a throwaway virtualenv in the
  scratchpad and is not added to `package.json`.
- Motion: ease-out / `--ease-luxury-bezier` only; no spring, no bounce.
- The pre-paint `sessionStorage` probe stays inline and render-blocking, and
  interpolates nothing.
- Keep each change in its own commit, so any one can be reverted alone.

## Security requirements

- No change to CSP (fonts are self-hosted; the film is still on
  res.cloudinary.com, already in `media-src`).
- No new inline script; no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` added.
- Font files stay under the OFL; the license files are kept.

## Acceptance criteria

- Home on a first visit: the intro lifts by **≤ 3.0 s** after navigation on a
  fast desktop connection (was 4.4–4.9 s); the sequence plays in full.
- A product, collection or journal page as the first page: **no intro**; content
  is visible at first paint.
- EN font transfer on the home page is **≤ 160 KB** (was 392 KB); AR is
  **≤ 300 KB** (was 432 KB). No tofu or fallback glyphs in EN or AR on home,
  product, checkout, account or the admin (spot-check prices, ◆, →, ×, Arabic
  diacritics in the heritage copy).
- The mobile home does not download the New Arrival film until it is scrolled
  near; the film plays when visible and pauses off-screen; under reduced motion
  it shows the poster.
- Cookie card: ≤ 30% of a 390×844 viewport; does not cover ADD TO CART on
  `/perfume/sunlit-citrine` at 1280×577; Accept, Decline and Manage still work
  in EN and AR (RTL places it bottom-left).
- Mobile Lighthouse Performance on home and product: **≥ 75**, CLS stays
  ≤ 0.05. Desktop scores do not drop.
- `tsc`, `eslint`, unit tests and `next build` pass.

   Arrival: its film starts loading and playing.

   cover ADD TO CART. On the phone it takes under a third of the screen. Accept,
   Decline and Manage preferences all work; the bag still fills after Accept.
6. Spot-check prices (EGP 1,470), the announcement bar's ◆ separators, the
   checkout and the admin dashboard for any wrong-looking characters.
